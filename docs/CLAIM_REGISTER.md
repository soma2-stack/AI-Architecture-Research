### Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED (within scope) | Existing independent or directly checked evidence establishes the exact limited claim at its pinned revision. Never a blanket theorem/architecture endorsement. |
| REFUTED | A specific assertion has an existing counterexample, failed decisive criterion, or incompatible contract. Preserve narrower surviving results. |
| PARTIAL | Some components are supported, but the stated broader conclusion or transfer is not established. |
| OPEN | A mathematical/scientific question remains unresolved. No automatic next action. |
| NOT RUN | The named test/stage was not performed in the documented scope. It is not an observed failure. |
| INVALID | A known implementation/protocol defect invalidates the specified result. Corrected work receives a separate status. |
| PENDING | Evidence or independent review is insufficient at the specified revision. It does not authorize an audit or rerun. |

Availability flags **NOT IN REPOSITORY** and **NOT AUTHORIZED** are separate from
claim truth. Lifecycle flags **STOPPED**, **ARCHIVED**, **RETIRED**, **WITHDRAWN**
and the C5 **NO-GO** govern whether work may resume. Old ACCEPTED/PROVED/SUPERSEDED
labels remain historical dispositions, not new independent verification.

## Why the central mathematical question remains open

PR37's verified variation identity bounds a pathwise response by normalized forcing
variation plus explicit initial/capture terms. Its common-query cross-history identity
retains a kernel term controlled by Gamma and associated variation. The existing
gate-energy/resource inequalities are too weak to provide the needed dimension-useful
bound in actual mT. Forcing depends on the recurrent history, and common maintenance
and source preparation still count even where input differences vanish. No current
result both closes that cost-to-full-metric gap and supplies a robust D=Omega(n)
independent section below the n^(3/2) boundary.

This is a record of the existing audit's limitation, **not an instruction to prove a
new bound, find a counterexample, design an architecture or run a numerical test**.
All such research remains frozen.
