# Research Rules

1. Generate broadly before narrowing.
2. Do not protect an idea because it sounds exciting.
3. Try to find prior work that invalidates novelty claims.
4. Record negative results.
5. Prefer testable mechanisms over vague concepts.
6. Separate architecture novelty from application novelty.
7. Do not call something a breakthrough without evidence.
8. Keep explanations understandable in plain English.
9. Preserve enough state that work can resume after context loss.
10. Continue exploring until there are several genuinely distinct candidates.
