# Scoped failures and unproved extensions

These classifications concern the new construction, not a reopening of
accepted results. No historical report is edited.

## FAILED: third-order determinant truncation

The raw derivative series through order three gives determinant coefficient
-eta^4/900. It is NOT the correct leading coefficient. Differentiated order
four contributes at the SAME determinant order, changing it to -eta^4/4500.
First failed inequality: an order-four Jacobian remainder O(eta^3) cannot
be dismissed compared with the determinant eta^4 after multiplying the
order-eta strong column. The new proof includes order four and an explicit
order-five-and-higher remainder.

## FAILED: treating two probe columns as two robust directions

Frobenius orthogonality alone gives no boundary-antipodal margin. The new
proof instead uses one fixed unit probe, TWO overlapping spatial reads,
an explicit continuum weak gain, and a uniform finite antipodal error.
It does not promote a full-column Jacobian rank to robust dimension.

## FAILED: reading a theta-dependent matrix's small singular value as a
finite antipodal upper

A matrix A(theta) with a small minimum singular value need not have small
A(theta)theta. Its weakest direction can vary with theta. The finite
constant-rate limitation in PROOF.md (26) instead applies Borsuk--Ulam to
the nonlinear polynomial-output approximation in one common public spatial
subspace. This step is essential, not optional.

## FAILED: extrapolating the new K=2 constant to growing K

First failed estimate in the constant-rate extension:

    sigma_min(J_K) <= 2 exp(ell) ell^K/K!.

The public survivor filters exp[-lambda_i(1-s)] share a very low-order
polynomial approximation when all rates stay in a fixed bounded interval.
After spatial centering, degree p has at most p dimensions. K=2 has a
nonzero but small high-order weak signal; K growing makes it factorially
ill-conditioned. No K-independent gain follows from the two-control result.

## CONDITIONAL / STILL OPEN: time-varying rate words

Public time-varying survivor words need not lie in the constant-rate
polynomial-output space. They might permit a better temporal filter bank.
What is missing is a quantitative finite-boundary minimum gain AFTER exact
trace matching, including the complete common renewal. No unproved filter
independence or improved K exponent is used in the phase diagram.

## CLOSED PREMISES, not retried

- One initial donor pulse plus public filtering: the accepted all-query
  bound .312delta/sqrt(n)+8e-9 prevents a robust large-width result.
- All-low constant-gap filters: credit cannot accumulate order-W magnitude.
- Identical survivor schedules: the private spatial response is one common
  factor. Our three distinct public rates avoid this specific collapse.
- Perfectly matched donor/survivor direct traces: the apparent signal is
  local and is erased by exact trace matching. The new survivor comparisons
  have public local traces and different private spatial responses.

None is a universal impossibility result for redesigned histories.
