partial results, not evidence that the whole mechanism is impossible.

## Smallest quantitative failure

The explicit unresolved sufficient obstruction is equation(32) in PROOF:
the all-legal-query diameter Gamma of H_N on equal local-code fibers must
be <=epsilon-16e-9. The currently proved generic bound .204N/sqrt(n)
does not imply this for N>>sqrt(n). A failure of that sufficient bound
would still not establish superlinear dimension; a full joint lower would
need the exact renewal system and uniform finite-radius antipodal margins.

The method-specific obstruction is not called a structural loss of all
common modes. The entire moving corridor remains open.
