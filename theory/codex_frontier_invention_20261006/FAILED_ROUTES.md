## Exact next unsolved lemma after this construction

The present R=2 coded route supplies O(m) dimensions with m=o(n). To obtain
linear/superlinear dimension at subcritical coordinate-time cost, one would
need more than a constant number of independently protected private row
vectors per donor allocation, without the old exponential Walsh retention
cost and without a new boundary-code or write-gain loss. This is not proved
here and is not implied by the number of available parameter columns.
