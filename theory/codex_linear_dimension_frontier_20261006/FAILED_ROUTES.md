# Failures, scope and first unresolved inequalities

THEORY ONLY. No failed route here is promoted to a universal theorem.

## 1. Extending the old long tail/mask to linear m — FAILED certificate

First failure: 10^8(log n+1)sqrt(m/n)<10^-6.
For m=cn with any fixed c>0 this expression diverges. This invalidates
that sufficient ledger, not the histories. Power 12 was not necessary:
the same inequality permits a smaller fixed logarithmic power with a
large onset or small prefactor.

## 2. Removing masks and keeping a common read — CONDITIONAL

The common row is not in the survivor zero-sum reducing space. During
the trace tail its exact recurrence includes sqrt(h_S)J_t. The reviewed
complete norm bound only yields O(log n sqrt(m/n)) relative movement.
Uniform protection cannot be inferred merely because the physical
endpoint is public. Early one-step capture avoids this unresolved step.

## 3. Early capture with m=cn under the strict budget — FAILED target

The legal early-capture theorem itself succeeds, with linear dimension
and actual pair margin>.3. The first target failure is the direct identity

    mT >= 2*10^8 n sqrt(m) = Theta(n^(3/2)).

It is not o(n^(3/2)), even though the fixed coefficient can be tiny. This
does not reverse the history-energy upper bound. It counts actual steps.

## 4. Any bounded-stage donor-control redesign — PROVED scoped obstruction

With R bounded and K<=m/2 groups, the history factors through RK controls;
Borsuk-Ulam gives D<=RK when the section's control map is continuous.
In the inherited constant-amplitude family those controls are recovered
continuously from realized early-write gates. Continuous dependence of
some arbitrary parametrization alone would not suffice. The complete
actual short-packet theorem requires
T>.020sqrt(n). Therefore D<25R mT/sqrt(n). These premises preclude linear
D at a little-o budget. The first remaining assumption to break is
bounded independently robust controls per active group, not log^12.

This theorem does NOT close complete moving-corridor chronology, whose
legal gate word may have mT controls and which need not factor through
bounded R times m amplitudes.

## 5. Weak fresh-bit masks — PROVED algebra, FAILED selector scaling

The masks can preserve old singleton coefficients uniformly with
contrast .005/R; no 2^(-R) survival loss is needed. But each new capture
is only O(kappa/R). Selecting one stage for its boundary lower needs
per-stage t=Omega(R n/sqrt(m)), hence total mT=Omega(R^2 n sqrt(m)).
At D=Theta(mR)=Theta(n), that certificate costs
Omega(n^(3/2) R^(3/2)). No claim excludes a different aggregate query
or a better joint temporal code.

## 6. Cohort-local captures — CONDITIONAL complete theorem

Older within-cohort balanced rows are exactly unchanged by another
cohort's mask. But a new capture ALSO leaves a between-cohort zero-sum
component. The first false shortcut is that it has zero read on all
later captures: a later unequal gate can convert that common component
into its balanced row. The complete transfer needs a chronological
comparison, not disjoint-support reasoning. Even an ideal diagonal
stage-selector model pays 1/sqrt(R) in capture and again in query support.

## 7. Gate-only lossless rotations — FAILED in that scope

For 0<=D<=I, norm preservation on H forces D to be the identity on H.
Thus legal diagonal gates cannot implement a nontrivial exact isometry
of stored rows by themselves. Lossy compensated rotations or a redesigned
use of fixed transport remain open.

## 8. Sequential hierarchy — FAILED ideal selector budget

Even granting constant-gain protected rows without any mask attenuation,
R separate stages of duration Omega(n/sqrt(m)) cost Omega(R n sqrt(m)).
With D=Theta(mR)=Theta(n), this is Omega(n^(3/2)sqrt(R)). This is an
early scaling test of the stated certificate, not a theorem for every
time-multiplexed history. Epoch count is not itself robust dimension.

## 9. Simultaneous temporal/spatial code — STILL OPEN

The exact missing inequality is a finite-ball all-query lower for
R jointly controlled repeated temporal coordinates per donor group
within ONE T=O(n/sqrt(m)) interval. It must hold on every boundary
antipode after exact traces, global front, reset and dense comparison.
Choosing R=ceil(log log n), m=Theta(n/R), would then permit linear D
with mT=O(n^(3/2)/sqrt(R)). We have neither a construction nor an
obstruction for that full claim. The existing failed matched-filter bank,
moving probes and single-pulse histories do not supply this inequality.
